## Hi there 👋 Welcome

We are the **ARU Tech Community**, a group of **students and technology enthusiasts at Ardhi University, Tanzania** who love learning, building, and sharing knowledge. Whether you are writing your first line of code or shipping real products, there is a place for you here.

---

## 🚀 About Us

* 🎓 A community of **Ardhi University students** from all programmes and years
* 💡 Passionate about **Software Development, Cloud Computing, AI, IoT, and GIS**
* 🌱 Learning together through **workshops, study groups, and hands-on projects**
* 🤝 Open to **collaboration** on Web, Android, and Data projects
* 🏆 Preparing members for **hackathons, internships, and tech careers**
* 📫 Reach us at **[your-community-email@gmail.com](mailto:your-community-email@gmail.com)**
* ⚡ Our motto: **Learn. Build. Share.**

---

## 🎯 What We Do

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| 🛠️ **Workshops** | Practical sessions on coding, databases, networking, and design |
| 👨‍💻 **Group Projects** | Real-world apps built collaboratively by members |
| 🏁 **Hackathons** | Internal and external competitions to test and grow skills |
| 🎤 **Tech Talks** | Guest speakers, alumni, and industry professionals |
| 📚 **Study Groups** | Peer learning for coursework and certifications |
| 🌍 **Open Source** | Contributing to and maintaining community repositories |
